## Intake rules
1. Compare candidate ID, normalized name + vendor, and registrable domain against `site-snapshot/data/ai-tools.json` and all earlier review batches.
2. Keep new or uncertain records at `verification_status=needs_review`; use `pricing_status=unknown` until official pricing, geography, quota and billing conditions are reviewed.
3. Cite official product, pricing, docs, API/feed and repository/license sources. Open the original source page; search snippets and third-party directories are discovery leads only.
4. Check license scope separately for core, enterprise folders, hosted service and bundled dependencies.
5. Do not infer free access from a trial, credits, open-source label or self-hosting option.
6. Do not scrape behind login, bypass robots/rate limits, or copy vendor marketing copy and logos.
7. Only an editor-reviewed record may be copied into the main catalog. Do not merge, deploy or publish automatically.
